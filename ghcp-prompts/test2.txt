•	Blocker = something preventing or seriously slowing progress. In agile/project work, blockers or impediments are things that stop or delay work from moving forward.
RLLOT example: Backend Unit Testing is still In Progress because the team is waiting for customer approval.
So instead of CTO seeing only:
Backend Unit Testing → In Progress
we could eventually show:
Backend Unit Testing → In Progress | Blocker: Customer approval pending 
•	Milestone = an important checkpoint we want to reach. Milestones are commonly used to group work and track progress toward an important point, often with a due date and completion percentage.
RLLOT example:
Milestone: All teams complete Phase 1 by September 30
or
Milestone: Vulnerability Discovery rollout completed for first 50 teams. 
•	Target = the number or result we want to achieve. KPI systems compare the current value against a defined target to show whether we are ahead or behind.
RLLOT example:
Current: 65% teams completed Phase 1
Target: 80% by end of month
Then leadership immediately knows there is a 15% gap. 
•	Data freshness = how recent the dashboard information is. Dashboards often show when data was last updated/refreshed so users know whether they are making decisions from current or stale information.
RLLOT example:
Last updated: 19 Aug, 4:30 PM
If a DR workbook has not been updated for 10 days, leadership can see that the status may be stale. 
•	Trend = whether something is improving, declining, or staying the same over time. KPI reporting commonly uses trends to help stakeholders identify movement or bottlenecks over a period.
RLLOT example:
Backend Unit Testing completion: June 40% → July 55% → August 72%
That tells the CTO progress is improving, rather than showing only today's 72%.
